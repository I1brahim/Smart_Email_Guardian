# Email Guardian landing

Mode: Persuade. New landing surface, with a user-pinned cyberpunk/glitch system.
Audience: classmates and a school-project reviewer exploring email classification.
Action: open the scanner, or load an editable synthetic message without sending it.
Build path: code-led implementation of the user's explicit visual and layout brief.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A neon message intercept invites a second look before clicking. The
landing makes the actual text-classification demo understandable without invented
accuracy, live protection, customer counts, or testimonials.

OWN-WORLD: Void black, green emission, magenta interruptions and cyan data lines.
Orbitron display, JetBrains Mono body and Share Tech Mono labels; thin technical
borders, cut corners, visible grids and a faint page-wide CRT texture.

STORY: Read the purpose, explore a synthetic intercepted message, open that
message in the scanner, and compare the two real model outputs with a caveat.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A full-width navigation rail; a 60/40 split with oversized left
headline and unmistakable launch action, and a right-offset interactive sample
terminal. A slanted capability rail overlaps its lower edge. Mobile prioritizes
the headline and action, followed by the functional sample preview.

FORM: The user's cyberpunk/glitch direction overrides the unrelated catalog
assignment; seed:21b1e430e4ec0abd. Composition and materials follow the explicit
brief. No generated comp or raster artwork is needed for this CSS-native world.

MOTION: Infrequent short headline corruption, a terminal cursor and slow border
emission; a shared pause control and reduced-motion media query stop all effects.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
